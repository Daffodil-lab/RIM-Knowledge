# 0. Purpose

This document records user-provided screenshots of **RimWorld Anomaly-style monolith structures** as a visual reference for the redesigned **Nexus Core**.

This is not a direct-copy asset plan.

The goal is to learn from Anomaly monolith readability:

- simple central mass;
- thick outline;
- heavy base;
- stage-based transformation;
- clear dormant/active/danger identity;
- strong central aperture or face;
- minimal but meaningful glow.

Shion Nexus should use these lessons while keeping its own identity: ceramic shell, cyan ledger control, blue-violet CVE depth, void-glass aperture, muted brass seams, and fox/clockwork subtlety.
