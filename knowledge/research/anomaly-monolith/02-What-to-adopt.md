# 2. What to adopt

Adopt structurally:

- one strong central block;
- side supports or buttresses;
- deep central recess/aperture;
- broad plinth/base;
- stage-based silhouettes;
- simple geometry at far zoom;
- strong black outline;
- one or two important glow lines;
- a sense of sealed power.

For Nexus Core, this translates into:

- Core Tower / sealed monolith silhouette;
- void-black central aperture;
- deep blue-violet CVE core glow;
- thin cyan ledger line;
- white ceramic or pale slab body;
- dark ruin-gray base;
- small fox-ear-like crown fins only if they do not clutter the silhouette.
