# 5. Shape options influenced by Anomaly monoliths

## 5.1 Sealed Monolith Core

Shape:

- central raised slab;
- broad lower platform;
- two short side supports;
- void aperture or sealed lens in the center.

Best for:

- strong ancient machine identity;
- avoiding religious tower look.

Risk:

- may look too much like vanilla Anomaly if red/pyramid cues are copied.

## 5.2 Core Tower with Side Buttresses

Shape:

- tower-like central mass;
- side buttresses inspired by archotech/Anomaly ruins;
- central void-glass lens;
- subtle crown fins.

Best for:

- current preferred Nexus Core direction.

Risk:

- too many side supports can make it look like a fortress gate rather than Core.

## 5.3 Sealed Core Gate

Shape:

- broad rectangular frame;
- central recessed vertical aperture;
- low platform and symmetrical side weights.

Best for:

- readable 3x3 Core if tower silhouette is too narrow.

Risk:

- can look like a doorway unless central lens is strong.
