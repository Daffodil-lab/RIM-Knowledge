# 3. What not to copy

Do not copy:

- red hostile glow as default identity;
- Anomaly horror tone;
- ritual altar impression;
- flesh/cult danger language;
- exact monolith geometry;
- pyramid symbol as direct design center;
- aggressive spikes as the normal state;
- red fault lines except for true Core danger.

Shion Nexus is not Anomaly horror. It is a quiet ceramic clockwork singularity.
