# 1. Why Anomaly monoliths are useful

Anomaly monolith visuals are useful because they communicate:

- a unique central object;
- dormant mystery;
- later-stage activation;
- danger escalation;
- ancient/archotech-like weight;
- state changes without needing many small details.

This is close to what the Nexus Core needs:

> The Core must be a single irreplaceable center whose state is readable at a glance.
