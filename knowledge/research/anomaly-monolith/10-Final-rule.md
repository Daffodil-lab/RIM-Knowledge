# 10. Final rule

Final rule:

> Use Anomaly monoliths as a reference for central-object weight and state escalation, not for horror identity.

The Nexus Core may borrow:

- monolith mass;
- heavy base;
- central aperture;
- stage readability.

It must keep:

- white ceramic shell;
- void-black aperture;
- blue-violet CVE depth;
- sparse cyan ledger control;
- quiet clockwork mercy tone;
- strict static-state performance limits.
