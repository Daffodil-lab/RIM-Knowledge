# 3. Human mutation, core creation, and division loop

## 3.1 Flesh Heart

A mutated human can become a one-per-map Flesh Heart that serves as the colony core and produces Hemogel passively.

Design lesson:

- A single strategic core gives the colony a clear heart and vulnerability.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Nexus Core is the irreplaceable strategic heart.
- Core destruction must permanently stop Nexus functions in alpha.
- Core should not be replaceable or mass-produced in alpha.

## 3.2 Colony Brain

A higher central structure can be created using large biomass and a human corpse, unlocking additional creatures and components.

Design lesson:

- A higher-tier central processor can gate advanced content.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Future Sub-Core, Named Registry, or higher Tower Ledger tiers can unlock later systems.
- Not alpha.

## 3.3 Synapse Core

Humans can be embedded to create a research-boosting structure that consumes Hemogel.

Design lesson:

- Converting a pawn into infrastructure can be powerful and memorable.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Shion Nexus should avoid forced pawn consumption in alpha.
- Future voluntary/ambiguous Mercy Connection should be handled carefully.
- Any research boost must have bounded cost and clear narrative consequence.

## 3.4 Division exploit

Infected humans can divide into two bodies, losing clothing and halving age and skills. Repeated division can push characters into child ages, allowing repeated growth-moment trait and passion rolls in vanilla RimWorld.

Design lesson:

- Transformation systems can accidentally expose vanilla lifecycle exploits.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Worker Terminals should not use ordinary child growth, romance, family, or aging systems in alpha.
- Terminal bodies should be temporary outputs with capped lifecycle.
- Do not create loops that reroll pawn traits or passions.

## 3.5 Flesh Cocoon

A cocoon can unlock flesh-related abilities in humans over time.

Design lesson:

- A chamber that gradually unlocks abilities gives strong progression identity.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Future Terminal specialization chambers may be useful.
- Alpha should not add multi-stage ability progression.
