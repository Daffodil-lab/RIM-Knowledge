Fleshbeast Colony is a strong reference because it fully commits to being a different colony ecology.

Shion Nexus should match that level of systemic identity, but not its biological horror form.

Final rule:

> Learn from the closed loop. Do not copy the flesh. Convert biomass ecology into ledger ecology: one Core, compact CVE, few active bodies, bounded records, readable state changes, and strict performance caps.
