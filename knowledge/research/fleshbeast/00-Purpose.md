# 0. Purpose

This document records the user-provided analysis of the three-part gameplay videos for **Fleshbeast Colony / フレッシュビースト・コロニー** and translates it into design lessons for **Shion Nexus**.

This is not a dependency plan and not a direct template.

Fleshbeast Colony is useful because it demonstrates a complete alternate-colony infrastructure: a central biological resource, territory floor, body-processing economy, role-specialized creatures, auto-defense, and colony-scale identity. Shion Nexus should learn from the structure, not copy the flesh theme or exploit-prone systems.
