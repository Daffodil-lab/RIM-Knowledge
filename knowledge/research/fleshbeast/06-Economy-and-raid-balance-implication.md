# 6. Economy and raid-balance implication

The reported gameplay impression is that many creatures and flesh buildings may have low or poorly counted colony wealth, allowing a tower-defense-like play style with fewer humans and large numbers of monsters or regenerative walls.

Design lesson:

- Alternate colony systems can accidentally become very strong if they do not participate fairly in vanilla raid wealth.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Shion Nexus should avoid hiding power from raid balance as a design goal.
- Alpha active bodies are hard-capped.
- Buildings should have reasonable cost/wealth unless testing shows balance problems.
- The fantasy should come from abstraction and limits, not from invisible wealth abuse.
