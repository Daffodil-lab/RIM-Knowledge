# 5. Buildings and infrastructure

## 5.1 Flesh walls and doors

Flesh walls/doors appear instantly from the ground when ordered and consume Hemogel. Flesh doors regenerate extremely quickly and can create very strong defensive perimeters.

Design lesson:

- Instant infrastructure is satisfying but dangerous for balance.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Nexus buildings can feel responsive through state changes and short pulses.
- Alpha should not add instant fortification spam.
- Shield Relay should remain a simple one-hit mitigation placeholder.

## 5.2 Spitter

A flesh-floor turret fires long-range acid, functioning like an organic mortar or artillery defense.

Design lesson:

- Faction infrastructure can include nonstandard defensive weapons.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Future defense nodes are possible.
- Alpha should not include artillery systems or automated turret networks.

## 5.3 Tentacle turret

A close-range tentacle defense stops enemies and may not count internally as a normal turret, avoiding vanilla turret-aware enemy pathing behavior.

Design lesson:

- Avoiding vanilla AI threat categories can be extremely powerful and can unintentionally break raid balance.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Shion Nexus should not deliberately bypass raid AI with hidden turret classifications in alpha.
- Defensive systems should be readable, limited, and not exploit vanilla targeting assumptions.

## 5.4 Flesh Leaf / Tree

Photosynthetic structures generate Hemogel from sunlight. Leaves can be fused into a more efficient tree, improving space efficiency.

Design lesson:

- Passive resource generation becomes interesting when space and upgrade compression matter.

Shion Nexus translation:

- CVE passive regeneration should be very slow and safe from zero.
- Future CVE amplifiers may exist, but alpha should avoid generator networks.

## 5.5 Fire suppression and pollution cleanup

Flesh infrastructure is vulnerable to fire, so dedicated biological fire suppression becomes necessary. Another structure can absorb pollution.

Design lesson:

- Strong infrastructure needs specific weaknesses and counter-infrastructure.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Future Nexus weaknesses could include Core vulnerability, CVE starvation, relay overload, and record degradation.
- Alpha already uses Core destruction and CVE shortage as the main limits.
