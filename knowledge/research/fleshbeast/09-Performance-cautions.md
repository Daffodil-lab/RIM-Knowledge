# 9. Performance cautions

Do not import these patterns into Shion Nexus without hard limits:

- auto-expanding territory;
- auto-capturing floor;
- automatic corpse scanning;
- automatic wild-animal lure loops;
- self-replicating combat units;
- split-on-damage units;
- instant defensive wall spam;
- hidden AI-classification defenses;
- uncontrolled body records.

Any future adaptation must specify:

- active pawn cap;
- tick frequency;
- map scan policy;
- cached registration path;
- save-data cap;
- raid wealth/balance treatment;
- failure mode.
