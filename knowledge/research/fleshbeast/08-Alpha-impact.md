# 8. Alpha impact

This reference does not change Alpha 0.1 scope.

Alpha remains:

- Nexus Core;
- Tower Ledger;
- CVE;
- Terminal Control Capacity;
- Worker Terminal;
- Minimal Tower Logistics;
- Shield Relay placeholder;
- Mercy Connection placeholder;
- Visual Animation Minimum;
- no DLC/HAR/old Shion Race dependency.

Fleshbeast Colony lessons reinforce the current alpha rules:

- simulate few bodies;
- use one central resource;
- keep the Core vulnerable;
- do not add uncontrolled creature/terminal loops;
- do not add corpse scanning;
- do not hide power from raid balance;
- keep systems readable through simple states.
