# 2. Core resource and territory system

## 2.1 Hemogel / biomass

Fleshbeast Colony appears to replace ordinary colony economy with **Hemogel**, a biomass-like resource used for creature production, flesh walls, flesh floors, and infrastructure upkeep.

Design lesson:

- A custom colony resource can make a faction feel like a different game mode.
- A resource loop is strongest when every major action reads through it.

Shion Nexus translation:

- CVE should remain the core operating resource.
- CVE must be scalar, compact, and wire-free.
- Unlike Hemogel, CVE should not become a corpse-farming economy in alpha.

## 2.2 Flesh Floor

Flesh Floor acts as territory and production-defense surface. It can expand from the core or be manually extended by spending Hemogel. It has negative beauty, but can automatically or manually capture animals and enemies that step on it, draining blood/nutrition without destroying the body.

Design lesson:

- Territory can carry gameplay identity.
- A floor can become more than decoration if it drives defense and production.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Tower Logistics can use compact channels and connected nodes as the Nexus equivalent of territory.
- Do not copy auto-capture floor behavior for alpha.
- Do not create hidden hostile-processing systems that scan the map.
- Shion Nexus should communicate control through ledger channels, not flesh terrain.

## 2.3 Mouth / Stomach

Dead bodies can be placed into a Mouth/Stomach structure to extract large Hemogel based on body size, including animals, humans, and rotten corpses.

Design lesson:

- A dramatic conversion building can make colony economy immediately understandable.
- Body-size-based yield is readable and intuitive.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Mercy Connection can later become a careful record-recovery or translation system.
- Alpha Mercy Connection should not be a corpse grinder.
- No automatic corpse scanning, no forced assimilation, and no full resurrection in alpha.
