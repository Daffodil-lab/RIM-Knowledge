# 5. Color translation from vanilla ruins to Shion Nexus

Vanilla-like archotech ruins often read through gray, olive, pale panels, and sparse glowing lines. Shion Nexus should shift that language as follows:

| Vanilla-like role | Shion Nexus translation |
| --- | --- |
| gray stone/metal mass | Blue-Black Navy or dark desaturated support. |
| pale ruin panel | Enamel White / Pale Ceramic Ivory. |
| yellow-green glow line | Pale Cyan ledger line. |
| ancient dark recess | Void Black lens or aperture. |
| hidden energy core | Vacuum Blue-Violet inner glow. |
| worn machine edge | Muted Brass seam or hinge. |

Do not keep the glow yellow-green as the main identity. It risks reading as vanilla circuit/power rather than CVE.
