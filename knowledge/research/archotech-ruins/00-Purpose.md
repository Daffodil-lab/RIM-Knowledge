# 0. Purpose

This document records the visual direction taken from user-provided screenshots of vanilla-style archotech ruins and translates it into Shion Nexus art rules.

This is not a direct-copy asset plan.

The purpose is to keep Shion Nexus visually compatible with RimWorld's existing ancient/archotech structure language while preserving Shion's own identity: cyan and blue-violet CVE, ceramic shell, void-glass core, muted brass seams, and ledger control.
