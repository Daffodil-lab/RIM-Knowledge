# 4. Three acceptable Core silhouette options

| Option | Description | Use case | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core Tower | 3x3 tower-like sealed monolith with crown fins and central aperture. | Preferred alpha Core. | Must not look religious. |
| Sealed Core Monolith | heavy rectangular/hexagonal archotech slab with dark lens. | Good if tower is too close to Monolyn. | Could look too generic without fox/CVE cues. |
| Core Pillar with Ledger Base | central pillar sitting on square ledger plinth. | Good for readable Core/Ledger connection. | More complex if over-detailed. |

Preferred alpha choice:

> Core Tower, but described as a Nexus Core, not as a prayer tower.
