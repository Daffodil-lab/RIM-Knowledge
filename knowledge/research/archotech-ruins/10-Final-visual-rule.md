# 10. Final visual rule

Final rule:

> Nexus Core should look like a quiet archotech-like ceramic Core Tower: simple, heavy, readable, and irreplaceable.

It should not look like:

- a crowded circuit board;
- a generic machine panel;
- a normal computer;
- a power battery;
- a Monolyn prayer tower;
- a Fleshbeast organ;
- an Arachnae nest.

The Core is allowed to look like a tower because it is the singular anchor of the colony. Its purpose must remain Core, not worship.
