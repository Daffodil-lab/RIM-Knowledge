# 3. Core visual direction change

## 3.1 Preferred alpha Core form

Preferred alpha form:

**Nexus Core Tower / 中枢塔**

Footprint:

**3x3**

Visual concept:

> A sealed white ceramic archotech-like tower with a dark blue-violet core aperture, subtle fox-ear crown fins, and a few cyan ledger lines.

This is still `Nexus Core / 中枢核` in gameplay terms, but the building may visually resemble a tower or monolith because the tower shape better communicates importance.

## 3.2 Shape language

Use:

- simple central vertical mass;
- broad dark base or plinth;
- pale ceramic main body;
- dark void-glass central lens;
- one or two fox-ear-like crown fins;
- one or two cyan ledger channels, not many circuit traces;
- limited brass seams;
- thick outline;
- optional side buttresses inspired by archotech ruins.

Avoid:

- dense circuit-board surfaces;
- many small yellow lines;
- flat rectangular equipment panel identity;
- generic computer terminal look;
- Monolyn religious tower copy;
- vanilla battery or power generator silhouette;
- cluttered high-tech console language.
