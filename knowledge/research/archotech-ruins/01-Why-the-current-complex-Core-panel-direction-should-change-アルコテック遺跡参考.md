# 1. Why the current complex Core panel direction should change

The previous Nexus Core visual direction risked looking like:

- a complex circuit-board panel;
- a generic equipment block;
- a wall-mounted control cabinet;
- a normal machine interface rather than the colony's irreplaceable heart.

The Nexus Core should instead read as:

> a simple, meaningful central object: tower, sealed monolith, core pillar, or archotech-like ruin anchor.

At RimWorld scale, a strong silhouette matters more than many interior lines.
