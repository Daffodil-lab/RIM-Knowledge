# 3. Performance review checklist

When reviewing the videos, note:

- whether the system appears to add many active pawns;
- whether routine work is abstracted or simulated visibly;
- whether base-wide processing appears continuous or event-driven;
- whether UI/readability is clear without many overlays;
- whether visual effects remain readable at normal zoom;
- whether player attention is directed by states, warnings, or animation;
- whether any design would be too expensive for Shion Nexus alpha.
