# 12. Final lesson

Monolyn is valuable because players actually build around its Tower, Light, baptism, and terminal systems.

The risk is that strong identity can become obscure onboarding, labor-tax economy, or hidden progress confusion.

Final rule:

> Learn from the Tower-centered play pattern. Do not copy the prayer burden. Shion Nexus should make CVE, capacity, terminal loss, logistics, and recovery visible through the Ledger, with hard caps and no hidden worker treadmill.
