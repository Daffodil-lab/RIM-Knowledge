# 10. Alpha impact

This reference does not change Alpha 0.1 scope.

Alpha remains:

- Core, Ledger, CVE, Worker, Minimal Tower Logistics, Shield Relay, Mercy placeholder;
- no DLC dependency;
- no HAR dependency;
- no Monolyn dependency;
- no schedule-based prayer labor;
- no cross-map Tower sharing;
- no conversion/recruitment system;
- no custom research UI.

The reference reinforces these alpha requirements:

- visible blocked reasons;
- inspect text for every state;
- no hidden progress that looks broken;
- clear capacity labels;
- terminal loss/recovery rules visible to the player;
- no resource treadmill that demands many permanent workers.
