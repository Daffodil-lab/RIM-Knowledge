# 5. Terminal body operation

## 5.1 Reconstruction instead of baptism resurrection

Reported player confusion:

- Destroyed mechanical terminal bodies cannot be revived through the baptismal font.
- They need `Terminal: Dominion` and its `Reconstruct` function.
- This is repeatedly asked/shared by players.

Design lesson:

- Separate recovery paths for organic conversion and mechanical terminal reconstruction must be explicit.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Worker Terminal death/expiry/recovery must be shown through Tower Ledger and Mercy Connection, not through unrelated systems.
- Alpha has one clear rule: expiry returns shell; death loses shell, adds recovery record, and damages Record Integrity.
- Future terminal reconstruction should use dedicated terminal recovery UI/commands, not Mercy Connection ambiguity alone.

## 5.2 Transmission capacity frustration

Reported player issue:

- Transmission capacity around 1200 is perceived as too low by some players.
- Developer has indicated future expansion may address this.

Design lesson:

- Terminal bandwidth/capacity systems strongly affect play feel.
- Too-low capacity causes frustration if players do not understand why it exists or how to expand it.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Alpha Terminal Control Capacity is deliberately small at 3, but this is a hard performance and identity cap, not a hidden grind.
- The cap must be displayed directly.
- Expansion is post-alpha.
