# 6. Base structure and expansion play

## 6.1 Tower-wide sharing and dual-base play

Reported player practice:

- Tower can share Light, materials, and personnel regardless of location.
- Players use this to run a main base plus minimal-personnel outposts, floating ships, or forward bases.
- NamuWiki review highlights dual-base operation as a strength.

Design lesson:

- Cross-location logistics identity can make a race/infrastructure mod feel powerful and unique.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Tower Logistics is a major reference.
- Alpha remains single-map/minimal logistics only.
- Future cross-map ledger logistics should be designed carefully with strict caps and clear transfer costs.

## 6.2 Odyssey / gravship play requests

Reported player practice:

- Players discuss using Monolyn with Odyssey gravship-style play.
- Requests exist for walls or support suitable for gravship bases.

Design lesson:

- Large infrastructure race mods are often played with major expansion mods/DLC and mobile-base play.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Alpha must not require Odyssey.
- Future mobile Nexus / ship support may be attractive, but it is expansion territory.
