# 8. The four real Monolyn management layers

The reported practical Monolyn loop appears to combine four resource-management layers:

1. prayer/meditation schedule management;
2. Light storage and income tuning;
3. baptism-based personnel growth and its costs;
4. terminal body loss/reconstruction management.

Design lesson:

- A strong identity mod can ask the player to manage multiple parallel layers, but every layer must be readable.

Shion Nexus translation:

Alpha should have fewer layers:

1. CVE value;
2. Terminal Control Capacity;
3. Terminal Shells;
4. Worker Terminal expiry/death;
5. one Relay charge;
6. Mercy Stock/recovery records.

These layers must be visible through Core, Ledger, Hangar, Relay, and Mercy Inspect strings.
