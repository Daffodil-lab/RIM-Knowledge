# 4. Baptism use and costs

## 4.1 Baptism as recruitment route

Reported player practice:

- Converting other races through a baptismal font is a basic way to secure Monolyn personnel.

Reported player confusion:

- Baptism may require black blood to fill first.
- Captive/prisoner state may prevent baptism.
- These restrictions confuse players.

Design lesson:

- Conversion systems require strict input-state explanation.
- Players need to know whether the blocker is resource, target state, or building state.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Mercy Connection and terminal output must return clear `NexusBlockReason` values.
- Alpha should not include enemy conversion.
- If future assimilation/translation exists, it must clearly display input eligibility and irreversible costs.

## 4.2 Psychic cost of conversion

Reported player concern:

- Baptism can remove or alter previous psychic capability/sensitivity.
- Players consider whether a valuable captive is worth converting.

Design lesson:

- Conversion is more meaningful when it has an opportunity cost.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Future Mercy Connection should preserve tradeoffs: record recovery, identity loss, abilities lost, or incomplete reconstruction.
- Alpha Mercy Connection already says not everything returns.
