# 7. Shion Nexus non-copy rules

Shion Nexus must not become:

- Monolyn with foxes;
- Fleshbeast with machines;
- Arachnae with cute terminals;
- a Biotech mechanitor replacement;
- an uncontrolled swarm mod;
- a full factory logistics game;
- a body-horror assimilation mod as the core tone;
- a visually noisy effect mod with no gameplay readability;
- a pawn facial animation dependent mod.

Shion Nexus must remain:

- one central clockwork fox doll;
- a Tower Ledger hive;
- terminal-body output under hard limits;
- concentrated vacuum energy instead of wires;
- logistics abstraction instead of pawn spam;
- core-defense first;
- merciful, technological, and morally ambiguous;
- animated where it communicates state.
