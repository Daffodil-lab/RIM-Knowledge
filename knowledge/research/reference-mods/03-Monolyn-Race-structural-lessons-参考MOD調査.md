# 3. Monolyn Race structural lessons

## 3.1 Useful structure

Monolyn Race is useful because it presents a complete central-resource colony model.

Publicly visible structure:

- Monolyns pray.
- Prayer gathers Light.
- Light is stored in the Tower.
- The Tower advances related research.
- Light enables instant construction and automated facilities.
- The system gives the colony a central sacred/technical infrastructure.

## 3.2 Shion Nexus adaptation

Shion Nexus should adapt this as:

- Nexus Core produces or stabilizes Condensed Vacuum Energy.
- Tower Ledger stores and displays central values.
- Tower Logistics routes materials through compact channels.
- Terminal Control Capacity limits active terminal bodies.
- CVE powers terminal output, emergency routing, Mercy Connection, and shield behavior.

## 3.3 Visual and animation lesson

Monolyn Race is also a presentation reference. Its Workshop page presents many preview images and states that dedicated mechanoids, dedicated weapons, entities, and various other things are added.

The useful reference is the overall presentation ambition, not pawn facial animation. `[NL] Facial Animation` is not needed for Shion Nexus and should not be adopted as a project target.

Shion Nexus should learn from this level of visual ambition:

- systems should have visible identity;
- major buildings should show clear active states;
- special resources should have readable visual feedback;
- deployment, recovery, shield, and mercy systems should feel distinct;
- presentation should make the mod feel complete, not just mechanically complete.

The adaptation must remain performance-safe:

> Animate important systems, not everything.

## 3.4 Design warning

Monolyn appears to make Light supply an important constraint. Player comments suggest that late-game Light demand can become heavy when many buildings exist.

Shion Nexus should avoid turning CVE into mandatory repetitive labor.

Correct adaptation:

> CVE should constrain spam, but not create permanent soft-lock or constant ritual workload.
