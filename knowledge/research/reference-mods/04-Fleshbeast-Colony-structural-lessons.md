# 4. Fleshbeast Colony structural lessons

## 4.1 Useful structure

Fleshbeast Colony expands Anomaly's fleshbeast theme into a colony-scale organic infrastructure. Its public description emphasizes living organ-like structures that grow, integrate into the base, and transform the settlement into a single biostructure. Hemogel is the central resource that nourishes the organic colony and sustains its life.

## 4.2 Shion Nexus adaptation

Shion Nexus should not become organic horror, but it can learn from the structure:

- the colony itself can be a system, not just buildings;
- infrastructure can feel alive or centrally coordinated;
- a central resource can feed the base state;
- expansion can imply transformation of the settlement;
- containment vs acceptance can become a gameplay tension.

For Shion Nexus, this becomes:

- Tower Ledger turns the colony into a recorded machine-hive;
- Mercy Connection processes remains, damaged terminals, and records;
- Core-defense infrastructure makes the base feel like an extension of Shion;
- CVE and Mercy Stock sustain special systems;
- transformation is technological and merciful, not fleshy and horrific.

## 4.3 Design warning

The organic-colony model can become too dominant and change the whole game into a different genre.

Shion Nexus should keep the tone:

- tender;
- technological;
- morally ambiguous;
- performance-conscious;
- core-defense focused.
