# 8. First playable reference priorities

## 8.1 High priority

- Monolyn-style central resource and Tower automation.
- Monolyn-style colony support through central systems.
- Monolyn-style presentation ambition for major systems.
- Arachnae-style role separation for terminal bodies.
- Performance-safe logistics abstraction.

## 8.2 Medium priority

- Fleshbeast-style infrastructure as colony transformation.
- Mercy Connection as non-horror transformation pressure.
- Emergency role adaptation for terminal bodies.
- Expanded animation sets for named records and terminal recall.

## 8.3 Low priority / expansion

- DLC-specific Anomaly interactions.
- Biotech optional compatibility.
- complex multi-base support.
- advanced terminal evolution.
- full logistics UI.
- elaborate cosmetic-only animation.
