| High visual presentation density | Monolyn preview assets and dedicated systems | Animated Core, Ledger, Hangar, Shield Relay, Mercy Connection, terminal deployment states. |
| Colony as integrated body | Fleshbeast organic infrastructure | Colony as clockwork/ledger hive infrastructure. |
