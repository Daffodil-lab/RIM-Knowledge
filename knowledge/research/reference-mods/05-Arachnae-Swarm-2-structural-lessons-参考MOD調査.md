# 5. Arachnae Swarm 2 structural lessons

## 5.1 Useful structure

Arachnae Swarm 2 publicly describes a Gestalt consciousness colony centered on a Queen. The Queen commands Overlords, and Overlords command Auxiliary Worms. The swarm has clear division of labor, can spread creep, produces eggs, grows soldiers quickly, uses Directed Evolution to transform into subspecies, and converges consciousness on the Queen.

## 5.2 Shion Nexus adaptation

Shion Nexus can learn from:

- central mind hierarchy;
- role-specific subordinate bodies;
- deployment from a stored production process;
- emergency growth or output;
- body role adaptation;
- non-individual identity flow back to the center.

For Shion Nexus, this becomes:

- Nexus Core as central identity;
- Tower Ledger as control and memory layer;
- terminal bodies as temporary role outputs;
- Worker / Guard / Engineer / Operator terminals;
- Named Terminal Registry for exceptional individuality;
- terminal loss returning as records rather than insect-like expendability.

## 5.3 Design warning

A swarm model can easily create too many active units.

Shion Nexus must not follow uncontrolled swarm expansion.

Correct adaptation:

> Many bodies are implied. Few bodies are active. Important bodies may become named records.
