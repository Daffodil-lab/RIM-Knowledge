# 8. UI/system failures to avoid

Avoid in Shion Nexus alpha:

- central actions with delayed invisible outcomes;
- progress that says inactive while active;
- similar items with indistinguishable descriptions;
- capacity buildings that do not state what they expand;
- custom research UI as a critical path;
- equipment display complexity for the Worker Terminal;
- non-fatal red errors treated as acceptable;
- missing `compClass` mistakes;
- compatibility-sensitive systems without fallback Inspect text.
