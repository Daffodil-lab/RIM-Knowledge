# 2. Overall system reception

Reported NamuWiki-style evaluation:

- Monolyn is high quality, but unusual compared with many existing mods.
- Its prayer UI, Shinjo-Codex dedicated equipment system, implant-based skill customization, and Protoss-like dedicated building/equipment production create a cool and mysterious atmosphere.
- The design itself is received positively as a distinctive integrated system.
- However, players also recognize unfinished parts and past uncertainty around whether development had stalled.

Design lesson:

- A complex system can be loved when it feels like a coherent faction ecology.
- Strong atmosphere can coexist with rough edges.
- Unfinished or unclear UI can make players doubt whether the mod is abandoned or broken.

Shion Nexus translation:

- Shion Nexus should keep the strong integrated identity: Core, Ledger, CVE, terminals, logistics, shield, mercy.
- Alpha should look intentionally limited, not accidentally unfinished.
- Every placeholder must say it is an alpha placeholder.
