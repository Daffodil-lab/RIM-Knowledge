# 12. Final lesson

Monolyn's system design is strong because its Tower, Light, Codex, implants, buildings, and terminals form a coherent identity.

Its weak point is not the concept. Its weak point is operational opacity: hidden progress, vague feedback, insufficient descriptions, research-tab conflicts, and implementation errors visible as red logs.

Final rule:

> Shion Nexus can be mysterious in lore, but it must be explicit in operation. Every Core, Ledger, CVE, terminal, relay, and mercy action needs visible state, exact failure reason, clear text, and a lightweight fallback.
